# Clear Harvest Market Growth Strategy

## Primary audiences

### 1. Truckee local regulars
They want a trustworthy place for better bread, juice, and bakery staples.

### 2. Tahoe visitors and weekend travelers
They need a stop that feels local, premium, and useful on the way to the lake, trail, cabin, or ski day.

### 3. Vacation rental hosts and guests
They need welcome baskets, breakfast boxes, and easy premium add-ons.

### 4. Local business / event buyers
They need catering trays, juice packs, boxed offerings, and recurring partnerships.

### 5. Social-first followers
They are ideal for preorder drops, flavor announcements, and recipe micro-product sales.
