# Clear Harvest Market Research - Truckee / Lake Tahoe First Pass

## Existing live-site signals preserved

From the current live site and brand footprint:
- Brand: **Clear Harvest Market**
- Region: Truckee, California / Lake Tahoe basin
- Current offer focus: cold-pressed juice, artisan sourdough, fresh baked goods, pre-orders
- Existing wording emphasizes real ingredients, handcrafted production, and community connection
- Existing social presence already includes Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok under `clearharvestmarket`
- Existing commerce path includes Hotplate ordering
- Current site says Truckee is home and references a permanent spot opening in Truckee, CA

## Existing brand story worth keeping

Current about-page story says the business began as a single booth selling homemade Almond Roca, expanded into cookies and fresh-baked bread, then evolved into cold-pressed juices. It positions the operation as a true family business built on real ingredients, honest products, and connection with customers.

That is good brand DNA. It should stay.

## Better positioning for growth

The upgraded site should position Clear Harvest Market as:

**Truckee's fresh-market bakery and juice stop for locals, visitors, cabin guests, and mountain-day travelers.**

That positioning is stronger because it captures:
- local repeat customers
- day-trip / weekend visitor traffic
- families stocking up before lake days or ski days
- vacation rental guests and hosts
- catering and hospitality opportunities
- take-home product sales beyond one-time walk-ins

## Demand fit in the region

Truckee and the Tahoe basin reward concepts that are:
- fast but high quality
- healthy without feeling sterile
- giftable and shareable
- useful for outdoors, cabins, and travel days
- clearly local and handcrafted

That means the strongest categories are:
- cold-pressed juices
- artisan breads and sourdough drops
- pastries and bakery case staples
- picnic and trail-ready market goods
- cabin breakfast bundles
- premium pantry / retail shelf items
- take-home recipes, kits, and seasonal specialty products

## Content angles for visitors entering the area

Visitors and part-time residents are often looking for:
- where to stop for breakfast before the mountain or lake
- what to bring to a rental house or cabin
- where to find a better local market than a generic convenience stop
- what to pick up for a picnic, hostess gift, or road-day provision run

That means site copy should speak to:
- "before the lake"
- "before the trail"
- "before check-in"
- "stock the cabin"
- "pick up something local on the way in"
- "fresh Truckee-made market goods"

## Recommended site strategy

### Homepage should do 5 jobs
1. Tell the brand story clearly
2. Explain what to order now
3. Speak to Truckee locals and Tahoe visitors both
4. Build the future permanent-store vision
5. Route people into ordering, recipes, socials, and partnerships

### Ordering strategy
Keep Hotplate as the live transaction path right now, while the website becomes the stronger brand and merchandising layer.

### Recipe strategy
Recipe micro-products at $1 each are smart because they:
- monetize the audience before the physical store fully scales
- reinforce culinary authority
- work well with email and social marketing
- can later expand into bundles, seasonal recipe packs, or ebooks

### Social strategy
Current site underuses socials. The upgraded site should make socials highly visible with reasons to follow:
- preorder drops
- new juice flavors
- sourdough flavor calendars
- market opening updates
- behind-the-scenes baking
- seasonal Truckee / Tahoe content
