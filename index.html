Her real story

What began as a single booth selling homemade Almond Roca kept growing.

That booth turned into cookies, fresh-baked bread, and now cold-pressed juices. After more than three years as a staple at Tennessee farmers markets, the same foundation stayed intact: real ingredients, honest products, and genuine connection with every customer.

It is a true family operation — from early mornings at the market to product development and the evolving in-house juice menu. Truckee has always been home, and that gives the brand a strong local anchor.

Why that matters now

This is exactly the kind of story a mountain-market brand should lean into.

Visitors want something local and real. Locals want something consistent and worth returning to. A family-built food brand with artisan bread, baked goods, and fresh juice has a much bigger ceiling than a plain preorder page.
