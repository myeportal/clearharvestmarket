Real story from the existing brand

Built from a single booth, grown through family hustle, and now rooted in Truckee.

What began as a single booth selling homemade Almond Roca grew into a full lineup of cookies, fresh-baked bread, and now cold-pressed juices. After more than three years as a staple at Tennessee farmers markets, the same foundation remains: real ingredients, honest products, and genuine connection with every customer.

This is a true family operation. From early mornings at the market to developing new products, every part of the business is hands-on. The juice menu, created in-house, reflects that same commitment to fresh, evolving offerings. At its core, Clear Harvest Market is about more than selling food — it is about knowing where it comes from, how it is made, and sharing that with the community. Truckee has always been home, and now the brand is here to stay.
