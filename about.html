Real story from the existing brand

Built from a market booth, grown through family hustle, and headed toward a bigger Truckee future.

Clear Harvest Market began with a single booth selling homemade Almond Roca. From there it grew into cookies, fresh-baked bread, and cold-pressed juices — all rooted in real ingredients, direct customer connection, and a family-run ethic.

That story matters. It gives the brand warmth, credibility, and a reason for people to come back that goes beyond just buying food.
